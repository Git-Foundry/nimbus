Key Prototype Capabilities

Predictive Operational Insights

Our system analyses real-time and historical aircraft data, anticipating potential gate conflicts or ground crew requirements. This provides airports with advance notice, allowing for proactive resource allocation and scheduling adjustments.

Turnaround Optimisation

The prototype offers tailored recommendations for each stage of aircraft turnaround, from arrival to departure. By integrating aircraft-specific metrics and airport conditions, it helps reduce idle times and improve throughput.

Fuel Consumption Analysis (Ground)

Nimbus monitors aircraft ground movements and engine usage, providing insights into fuel burn during taxiing and parking. These actionable recommendations aim to support more sustainable and cost-effective ground operations.

Real-time Anomaly Detection

The prototype continuously monitors aircraft data streams for unusual patterns or deviations during ground operations. Early alerts allow for swift intervention, mitigating potential safety risks before they escalate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What data sources does Nimbus utilise?

Our prototype processes diverse datasets including flight plans, aircraft sensor readings, ground movement tracking, and airport infrastructure status. We focus on secure and compliant data integration.

How does Nimbus integrate with existing airport systems?

As a prototype, our initial integrations are designed for modularity. We aim to connect with common operational data feeds and can discuss specific system compatibility during early access consultations.

What are the security measures for data handled by Nimbus?

Data security is paramount. Our development adheres to robust encryption standards and access protocols, ensuring sensitive operational information remains protected and private.

Is the Nimbus prototype suitable for any size airport?

The modular nature of our AI allows for scalability. We believe the core insights provided by Nimbus can benefit airports of various sizes, with specific configurations tailored to operational scale.
